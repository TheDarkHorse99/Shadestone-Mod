# Umbral World

A dark, gothic-horror dimension mod for **Minecraft Java Edition 26.3** (Fabric).

Under two black suns, the Umbral World is a fog-choked place where light is scarce and the things that live there fade from sight the moment light touches them. Build a portal of Shadestone in the dark, or fall out of the bottom of the world, and find out what's waiting.

> **Alpha.** This is an early version. Expect bugs and balance changes, and keep backups of worlds you care about.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Java Edition **26.3**
- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) **0.19.5** or newer
- [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for 26.3

## Installing

1. Install Fabric Loader for Minecraft 26.3 with the Fabric installer.
2. Download `umbral-world-<version>.jar` from the [Releases](https://github.com/TheDarkHorse99/Shadestone-Mod/releases) page.
3. Put it, together with Fabric API, in your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
4. Start Minecraft with the Fabric profile.

## Getting there

- **The portal:** build a frame of Shadestone blocks, shaped like a Nether portal. It opens by itself in the dark, at light level 4 or below, so it works at night but not in daylight.
- **The void:** fall out of the bottom of the Overworld or the End, and you'll land in the Umbral World instead of dying.

Shadestone Ore can be found deep in the Overworld, near bedrock.

## What's inside

- **Three biomes:**
  - the **Grave**, a misty graveyard wasteland
  - the **Miasma**, a sinister fairy forest of ghostly trees and plants whose auras sap your strength
  - the **Chasm**, a dried-up seabed split by a web of bottomless fissures with dark water far below
- **Creatures** that turn invisible in the light, including the towering Wight, the skittish Ghoul with its glowing eyes, the Crawler, the wall-phasing Cursed Soul, and the Murkfiend and Chasm Skate lurking in the Chasm's water
- The **Shadestone Blade**, whose hits cause Deep Blindness
- The **Dark Furnace**, which smelts using darkness instead of fuel
- **Corpse Candles**, the Wight's Shroud, Ectoplasm and the Potion of the Nightstalker, and the rare Cinderite ore
